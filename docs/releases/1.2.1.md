No beta or prerelease labels. The accepted application binaries are unchanged.

## Changes

- Windows and Linux share the desktop sources, including Rutube channels and
  show collections, source-aware archives, scheduling and limits.
- Recovery after interrupted downloads and subsequent download starts retains
  the fixes accepted on Linux.
- Windows bundles Python, yt-dlp, FFmpeg/FFprobe and Deno. Linux DEB uses its
  declared distribution dependencies; Deno is optional and not bundled.
- Android: `1.2.1`, version code `120100`, minimum Android 8/API 26. Four R2 APKs
  use the permanent release certificate. Controlled Python/FFmpeg runtimes,
  Cryptodome, Mutagen and the MediaNDK loader correction are included.
  Download recovery, encrypted transfer and archive relinking fixes are retained.
- Ten translated READMEs include refreshed desktop screenshots and Android
  screenshots in dark theme.

## Sources, Licenses And Integrity

Platform-specific SHA256SUMS files and `SHA256SUMS-1.2.1.txt` provide integrity
checks. `RELEASE-MANIFEST-1.2.1.json` records exact files and publication approval;
Android BUILD-INFO and SOURCE-REVIEW retain the original APK/source bindings.

Android application sources and 957 original third-party notices accompany the
APKs. The complete reviewed R5 runtime-source bundle is retained in the
[stable Android release](https://github.com/LiberVixer/YouTubeHarvester/releases/tag/android-v1.2.1):
[runtime sources](https://github.com/LiberVixer/YouTubeHarvester/releases/download/android-v1.2.1/YouTubeHarvester-1.2.1-runtime-sources.tar.gz).
SHA256: `c6b90687e95251082a1a261053aa4ba2bfcb302edd2099261c45868877cf907f`.
The exact R2 rebuilding payload is also retained there. The Android application
is GPL-3.0-only; third-party components retain their original licenses.

The maintainer confirmed working builds and approved final publication.
Automated reports retain their actual test scope and deferred device checks;
approval does not mark unrun tests as passing or claim byte-identical rebuilding
on every toolchain.

For Android, choose the APK matching your device ABI. A permanent-key APK cannot
overwrite a differently signed old tester: preserve/export data before any
uninstall. Future releases retain the permanent certificate.
